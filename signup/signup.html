What's in each email

  • What changed this week: who's getting attention, who's gone quiet, and which capabilities are being talked about.
  • Key moves: competitor launches, partnerships, funding, leadership changes and accuracy claims.
  • Worth reading: the LinkedIn posts and articles drawing the most discussion.
  • Beyond the watchlist: news from companies we don't track yet, plus what industry voices and researchers are publishing.
  • A link to the full report, with views from the last week to 90 days, competitor momentum, and every item found.

Built from public sources only. Items are tagged automatically, so check the original before citing anything.
